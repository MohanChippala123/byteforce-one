Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This site is a personal project. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. This page explains the small amount of data involved in running it.

Access cookie

After you enter the correct passcode, the site sets one cookie named bf_access. It contains a value proving you unlocked the site, is HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax, and ends when your browser session ends. It contains no personal information and is not used for tracking.

Checklist progress

Checkbox progress is saved in your own browser using localStorage under keys such as byteforce:checklist:ubuntu. It never leaves your device. Use the Reset button or clear site data in your browser to remove it.

Session storage

The current browser tab uses sessionStorage to remember in-site navigation and the wrong-passcode count. These values are cleared when the tab is closed.

Server logs

The site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel processes standard request data such as IP address, user agent, path, and time in order to serve the site, apply rate limits, and keep it secure. Failed passcode attempts are counted in memory per IP for up to 10 minutes, and the firewall rate limit blocks excessive requests for a short period. These logs are not sold or used for advertising.

Sharing

Data is not sold or shared for marketing. It is handled only by the hosting provider as needed to run the site, or where required by law.

Your choices

You can clear the site's cookie and browser storage at any time through your browser settings. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the site.

Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Contact
